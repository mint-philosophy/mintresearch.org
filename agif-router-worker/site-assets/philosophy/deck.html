What philosophy can contribute

Conceptual hygiene

Avoid overloading definitions or trying to win arguments by definitional fiat. Make disagreements clear.

Normative criticism

Understand what our criticisms amount to, flesh them out substantively, and distinguish substance from vibes.

Goals and constraints

Clarify what we should aim for and the constraints on pursuing it.

Limitations

Philosophy itself is not especially helpful for figuring out how to get from here to there.
