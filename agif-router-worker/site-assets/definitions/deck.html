Definitions

Professor, JHU SGP

The philosopher’s favourite part…

Metaobservation: terminological anxiety is a waste of time. As Frank Jackson once proposed: “Distinguish… and Relax”.

Should we retire “AGI”?

No! Just be precise about what you mean. *And* talking about AGI makes it immediately vivid that you’re *not* talking about business-as-usual AI, it removes scope for structured ambiguity. Something big and distinctive is happening. Be clear that this is what you’re talking about.

Definitions of AGI

Hassabis’ version

Mechanistic/Cognitive

≥ Human level at all cognitive functions, e.g. language, perception, planning, reasoning, memory, commonsense, social/moral cognition, metacognition etc

“Jagged frontier” but progress on all fronts. But what about non-human cognitive functions? And more broadly align mixes?

Legg and co

Capabilities

Focus on behaviours not cognitive capacities. Domain-general, ≥ expert human level, autonomous (able to succeed at its goals w/o human supervision)

More about what AGI can do than what it is. Does seem possible to hill-climb on a wide range of areas without genuinely being *general* intelligence

Outcomes

Outcomes

Alternative approach looks not at what individual systems can do but at the overall impact on society, e.g. Transformative AI, powerful AI

Outcome-based approaches allow for strongly divergent interpretations. Narrow AI was already transformative and powerful.

Normal Technology?

Is AI intrinsically normal? We are investing dumb matter with agency, and perhaps spirit. So I’m a hard “no” on this one.

Is it, like other technologies, subject to societal friction?

Maybe define powerful AI by extension not intension?

Locked in

Agents

Behavioural AGI is very close. Most capable models + harnesses can do significant proportion of computer-based work. Multi-agent swarms perhaps a new level?

Key ongoing defects: memory and creativity

On the horizon

Science

Within-paradigm progress from AI in many different fields (AI research, mathematics, biology) already feasible. Paradigm-shifting not yet.

Creativity again the key bottleneck

Can’t rule out

Independent ASI

Autonomous superintelligence. Plausibly beyond current architectures, but don’t bet against it.

Surprisingly few Rubicons remain

What about robots?!? Not so much progress there, albeit not *none*, and don’t snooze on AI agents controlling dumb swarms of drones…

Possible Bottlenecks

01

Verifiability

Are we only going to get progress in verifiable domains?

02

Generalisation

Is the progress we make always going to be limited to the training distribution? Will we never achieve real generalization?

03

Creativity

When it comes to genuinely original thinking, will models be constrained by the limitations of their training data and methods?
