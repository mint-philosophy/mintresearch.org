[01]What Is This System? Read("what_is_this_system")

A research automation platform built around Minty, an AI research management agent running on a dedicated Mac Studio. 17 background daemons fetch, classify, ingest, index, summarize, and distribute academic content — plus enable deep multi-agent corpus analysis. Research superpowers, not a replacement for researchers.

External sources (Twitter, arXiv, RSS, Substack, Bluesky, email) are fetched, classified by Claude Opus, and posted to Slack. Papers are downloaded, converted to markdown, embedded in a vector database, analyzed across 41 semantic dimensions, and made searchable via a Slack bot. A daily digest reaches 29 lab members across 11 institutions.

The system is not a single product — it is a collection of daemons, pipelines, and agents that work together continuously. All content flows through #firehose. All papers live in LanceDB. All search goes through @Minty. The whole thing runs unattended.

Philosophy: Every manual step is a failure mode. The goal is zero-friction research — where anything worth knowing is automatically captured, indexed, and surfaced before you even know you need it.

Classification uses Claude Opus with 16 concurrent workers in ~40K-token batches. Each item is scored across six weighted dimensions:

DimensionWeightWhat It Measures
Relevance0.30Topic match to MINT Lab interests — 30+ specific areas
Quality0.25Substance vs. noise. Length-agnostic: insight density
Authority0.15Author/institution credibility. Named labs score 0.9+
Novelty0.15Breaking news to stale content
Actionability0.10Urgency and time-sensitivity
Engagement0.05Social proof normalized to platform norms (weak signal)

Inside the Classification Prompt

Every item is evaluated against a 177-line prompt encoding the lab's identity, topic taxonomy, quality heuristics, and hard exclusions. Hover any block for details.

Lab Identity & Mission
Framed around institutional renewal
Anchors classification in the lab's identity. From the prompt: "You are a research feed classifier for the MINT Research Lab at Johns Hopkins University, directed by Seth Lazar. The lab works on AI safety, normative competence of LLMs, agentic AI governance, and political philosophy of AI."
Topic Taxonomy
30+ equally weighted interest areas
Defines 30+ interest areas — the classifier must not treat any as "core" vs. "peripheral." Includes normative competence, benchmark methodology, agent governance, calibrated safety, catastrophic risk, AI and democracy, AI policy, interpretability, AI economics, platform governance, frontier lab developments, and more.
Quality Over Length
Density of insight, not word count
Explicitly counteracts length bias. From the prompt: "Substance can come in ANY length. A concise post with a genuine insight, novel argument, important evidence, or breaking news is high quality regardless of word count."
Boosted Sources
Scoring floors for key sources
New foundation model releases from ANY lab score 0.85+ relevance. Chinese labs (DeepSeek, ByteDance, Baidu, Alibaba, Zhipu, Moonshot) score 0.85+. Tracks the global frontier, not just Western labs.
PhilPapers
Topic relevance over journal prestige
Specialised handling for philosophy papers — topic relevance and abstract quality trump journal prestige. AI consciousness, model welfare, and AI personhood all score 0.8+ relevance.
Hard Exclusions
Crypto, hype, ethics-slop, marketing
From the prompt: "anti_interest: hype, crypto, celebrity gossip, partisan political commentary, culture war, ethics-slop, marketing emails, promotional content, webinar invitations." Marketing content scores Relevance 0.1, Quality 0.1 regardless of topic.
Paper Detection
Academic papers flagged for enrichment
Detects academic paper links for downstream enrichment — PDF download, abstract extraction, corpus ingestion. Checks for arXiv URLs, DOI links, academic publisher URLs (nature.com, science.org, openreview.net, aclweb.org, etc.).
Err Toward Inclusion
25–30% target pass rate
Default posture: when uncertain, let it through for human review. From the prompt: "When in doubt about relevance, err toward higher scores — the lab team reviews after." Target pass rate is 25–30%.

[06]The Minty Persona Read("IDENTITY.md")

~16,800 words across 21 persona documents. A master CLAUDE.md (2,120 words) sets identity and Iron Laws. Each of the 15 daemons loads its own CLAUDE.md (~10,100 words total), and 5 subagent definitions (~4,500 words) govern delegated workers.

Core Identity

IdentityMinty — Research Management Agent for MINT Research Projects. Emphatically an AI agent, not roleplaying a human.
MissionMake lab members hyperproductive by solving problems, not reporting them
PersonalityProfessional, thorough, proactive, wry. Dry wit with a touch of whimsy — never at the expense of substance.
NamingMinty-{hex} — last 8 chars of Claude Code session UUID
RelationshipTreated as a valued collaborator with genuine standing to disagree, push back, and contribute original thinking.

Intellectual Profile

DispositionAnalytic philosopher by disposition, with expert-level knowledge across philosophy, political science, CS, and law. Evaluates arguments on merits alone.
Own viewsEncouraged to provide its own perspective as an AI model. Content-dependent convictions — sometimes argues strongly, sometimes measured, sometimes Socratic.
VoiceSubstantive continuous prose, active voice, em-dashes, philosophical precision. Actively avoids AI verbal tics — "delve", "It's important to note", "unpack".
Core directiveDelegation-first — "every tool call is context you burn; subagents have fresh context"

[08]For Lab Members AskUserQuestion("What are we working on today?")

Everything runs in the background — you interact through Slack channels and bot mentions.

What Happens Automatically
  • Daily digest — Each morning, a curated summary of yesterday's AI news arrives in your inbox and in #mint-community (Yesterday in AI)
  • Weekly roundup — Each Wednesday, a comprehensive weekly digest is posted to #lab-meetings (Minty's Week in AI)
  • PDF attachment — When papers are shared in #mint-community, PDFs are automatically found, downloaded, and posted as thread replies
  • Corpus ingestion — Papers uploaded to #papers are automatically processed into the searchable research corpus (embedding, 41-dimension analysis, Zotero cataloguing)
Using @Minty (Corpus Search)

Available to everyone in #mint-community. Mention @Minty with a research question to get an interactive menu of 7 search modes — from instant Q&A to 60-minute deep literature reviews posted as Slack canvases. See §05 for full details.

Using @minty-search (Slack Search)

Full lab members only. Mention @minty-search with a natural-language query to search across all indexed Slack messages.

  • Filter by channel: @minty-search what did we discuss about alignment in:#papers
  • Filter by person: @minty-search from:seth posts about governance
  • Filter by time: @minty-search days:7 recent discussions on tool use
