Thinking Machines puts a dial on how hard Inkling thinks

The controllable thinking-effort setting lets developers trade tokens for score: Inkling matches Nemotron 3 Ultra on Terminal Bench 2.1 at roughly a third of the tokens, and the company pitches the open-weights model as a base for customization.

The Minty Newsroom · Edited by Fable · 16 July 2026 273 words · ~2 min

Thinking Machines has detailed how Inkling, the open-weights multimodal model it released for fine-tuning, trades reasoning depth for speed and lower cost. Its launch thread and announcement describe controllable thinking effort, a setting that moves the model along a cost-performance curve by spending more or fewer tokens on reasoning. Sweeping that effort from 0.2 to 0.99 across Terminal Bench 2.1, Humanity's Last Exam, and IFBench, the company shows Inkling reaching a given score with fewer generated tokens than models including GLM-5.2, Kimi K2.6, Nemotron 3 Ultra, and GPT-OSS. On Terminal Bench 2.1 it matches Nemotron 3 Ultra at roughly a third of the tokens.

In the announcement, Thinking Machines calls Inkling "not the strongest overall model available today, open or closed," and pitches it as "a good open-weights base for customization," resting the case on multimodal capabilities, efficient thinking, and fine-tuning on Tinker. At effort 0.99, Inkling scores 63.8% on Terminal Bench 2.1, 79.8% on IFBench, and 29.7% on the text-only Humanity's Last Exam, a figure the company notes reflects an earlier checkpoint. On audio, the launch thread ranks it among the strongest open-weights models, citing VoiceBench, MMAU, and AudioMC.

Thinking Machines pretrained Inkling on 45 trillion tokens of text, images, audio, and video; the model activates 41B of its 975B parameters per token and carries a 1M-token context window. The company also previewed Inkling-Small, a 276B model with 12B active that it says matches or exceeds its larger sibling on many benchmarks. Inkling is on the Tinker fine-tuning platform now, with full weights on Hugging Face, including an NVFP4 checkpoint for NVIDIA Blackwell hardware. Inkling-Small remains a preview, with weights to follow.
