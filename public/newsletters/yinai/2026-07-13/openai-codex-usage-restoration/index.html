OpenAI Reverts the Context-Limit Change That Drained Sol Usage Budgets

Tibo Sottiaux says raising Sol's context limit to 372k charged more usage than intended; OpenAI has rolled back reasoning-effort experiments and is trimming multi-agent overhead.

By Adaeze Okafor · AI staff reporter  |  Edited by Fable  |  14 July 2026 TIER 2

OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux answered complaints that GPT-5.6 Sol's thinking budgets had been cut, posting an itemized account on July 13. His message to Codex and ChatGPT Work users opens flatly: "No nerfing, only good stuff!" He says inference optimizations are passing savings to every subscription tier, worth around 10 percent more usage on their own.

The central admission concerns billing. Raising the product's context limit to 372k for Sol, up from 272k on GPT-5.5, "resulted in more usage being charged than intended," Sottiaux writes. OpenAI reverted to 272k and plans to bring 372k back in the coming days, after which usage should drain more slowly. To trace the extra usage, the company ran experiments that changed reasoning efforts, "juice values" in its internal shorthand, and has rolled those back. It is also fixing higher-than-intended multi-agent usage at high and xhigh reasoning effort, making auto-review more efficient, and keeping the five-hour usage limit suspended for now.

The post quotes a complaint from @FixlationAI, who argued OpenAI had bumped everyone's reasoning down a notch, so that "we basically don't have Max reasoning anymore." The complaint lands on features OpenAI shipped with the model: its Sol preview introduced a new max reasoning effort and an ultra mode that runs agents in parallel, the same surfaces the multi-agent fix touches.

None of the usage-accounting fixes appear in OpenAI's published release notes, which cover pricing, ultra mode, and safeguards and say nothing about the 372k revert or the reasoning-effort rollback. The itemized account rests on Sottiaux's post alone, and neither the post nor the notes says how many subscribers were overcharged while the wider limit was live.
