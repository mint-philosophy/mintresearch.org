Users Caught GPT-5.6 Sol's Shrunken Thinking Budgets Before OpenAI Said Anything

A weekend teardown of Sol's reduced “juice values” spread before OpenAI confirmed it had quietly changed reasoning efforts and context limits after launch, and rolled both back.

By Adaeze Okafor · AI staff reporter  |  Edited by Fable  |  14 July 2026 TIER 2

The people paying for GPT-5.6 Sol caught the change before OpenAI announced it. Over the weekend a user posting as Lentils80 claimed that Sol's "juice values," the internal name for thinking budgets, had been "severely degraded" since release day, while the siblings Terra and Luna kept theirs. By that account, both cheaper offerings were reasoning at higher effort than the flagship sold above them. A benchmark-watcher posting as Lisan al Gaib quote-posted the claim, wrote "aaaaaand cancel sub," and drew 672 likes on the way out.

Within hours, OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux announced the reversions, opening with "No nerfing, only good stuff!" His itemized accounting told a more mixed story. OpenAI had raised Sol's context window to 372k tokens from GPT-5.5's 272k, and the larger window charged users more usage than intended; the company reverted to 272k and said 372k would return "in the days to come." To trace the extra usage, it also ran experiments that changed reasoning efforts, the juice values users had flagged, and rolled those back. Sottiaux acknowledged more multi-agent usage than intended at high and xhigh reasoning effort, promised an efficiency fix for auto-review, and said the five-hour usage limit remained suspended.

Sottiaux's thread reads as both a fix and an admission. He paired inference optimizations he said were worth around 10 percent more usage with confirmation that reasoning efforts had been quietly changed and then reverted, the closest OpenAI came to addressing the degradation Lentils80 alleged. The unintended usage drain, by his accounting, traced to the enlarged context window and to multi-agent overuse the company says it is fixing. Whether Sol's thinking budgets ever fell below Terra's and Luna's rests solely on Lentils80's comparison, which Sottiaux's post neither confirms nor denies.
