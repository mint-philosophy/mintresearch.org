‘A total mess’: Evans and Gruber pan the new ChatGPT super app

Benedict Evans cannot tell a project from a task from a chat; John Gruber says the redesigned app ships OpenAI’s org chart, with researchers ascendant and designers unheard.

By Adaeze Okafor · AI staff reporter  |  Edited by Fable  |  14 July 2026 TIER 2

Benedict Evans opened a Threads post on the redesigned ChatGPT app with five words: “Wow, what a total mess.” His complaints ran specific. He could not tell a project from a task from a chat; chats opened in a floating window while tasks and projects did not; and choosing “plugins” delivered “templates.” He asked whether he was even allowed to finish “setup” without connecting Slack or Google Drive. The post drew 19,000 views and a reply thread heavy with agreement.

John Gruber linked the post on Daring Fireball and invoked the old observation that companies dominated by internal politics ship their org charts. “That’s never been truer than with the new ChatGPT app,” he wrote. The old app “was focused,” still ships on mobile, and survives on some Macs as “ChatGPT Classic”; the new one carries “the veneer of a polished app” without the structure to match. “It’s playing dress-up as a big-boy app,” he added. Gruber grounded his diagnosis in secondhand reports, his “understanding from people adjacent to OpenAI”: the real clout inside the company belongs to the AI researchers, and product designers go unheard. He also admitted he forgot how he made the setup bar disappear despite never connecting Slack or Google Drive.

The reply thread supplied its own texture. One commenter described onboarding during which “a slack login browser window” appeared “with no prompting or indication that it came from Codex.” Another wrote that Codex had been the first OpenAI product that “felt like somebody working in access actually had some input,” then called that quality gone. The evidence stays thin throughout: one power user’s account of his own screens, plus a claim Gruber himself flags as secondhand. Whether OpenAI intends to revisit the projects-tasks-chats hierarchy goes unaddressed in both posts.
