Kimi K3 buys a 13-point intelligence jump at triple the cost

Artificial Analysis scores the new proprietary reasoning model 57 on its Intelligence Index, fourth in its class, but the step up from K2.6 arrives with $3/$15 pricing, below-average speed, and 130 million tokens of verbosity.

The Minty Newsroom · Edited by Fable · 17 July 2026 352 words · ~2 min

Artificial Analysis has published its measurements of Kimi K3, the proprietary reasoning model released July 16, and the figures describe a capable system that is expensive to run and unusually talkative. On the firm's Intelligence Index v4.1, which aggregates nine evaluations including GDPval-AA v2, Terminal-Bench v2.1, SciCode, Humanity's Last Exam, GPQA Diamond, and the long-context test AA-LCR, K3 scored 57, placing fourth in its class against a median of 30 for comparable reasoning models. The model carries 2.8 trillion parameters and a one-million-token context window, reads text and images, and writes text.

In a post on X, Artificial Analysis paired the score with a cost warning, calling it "a massive 13 pt increase in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index over K2.6" while putting the price at roughly triple the earlier model's. K3's API pricing runs $3.00 per million input tokens and $15.00 per million output, against medians of $1.75 and $8.40. Measured as cost per Intelligence Index task, K3 lands at $0.94: about half of Claude Opus 4.8 at maximum reasoning ($1.80), close to GPT-5.6 Sol at maximum ($1.04), and near triple Kimi K2.6 ($0.33) and Grok 4.5 on high ($0.31).

Artificial Analysis's accounting shows where the money goes: over the full index run, K3 generated 130 million output tokens, roughly double the 63-million-token median, behavior the firm labels "very verbose." Since every one of those tokens bills at the $15 output rate, running the whole index cost about $2,700. A cache-hit price of $0.30 per million tokens, a 90 percent discount, lowers the cost of workloads that resend the same long context.

K3 decodes at 62 output tokens per second against a median of 70 for its price tier, though its 1.99-second time to first token beats the roughly 2.6-second median. That mix leaves the model fourth for intelligence on Artificial Analysis's leaderboards but 88th for speed and 127th for price. It reaches users through a single API provider, and its weights are not public. The firm's summary calls K3 one of the leading models in intelligence, somewhat expensive next to models of similar price, and slower than average.
