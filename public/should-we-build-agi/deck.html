01 — Introduction

Why the question matters

Is AGI simply inevitable?

“AGI is inevitable” is implausibly deterministic.

AGI is a very physical technology. In an age of long-range precision weaponry, it is not beyond collective choice.

02 — Introduction

Why the question matters

Even if AGI were inevitable…

1

Should we try to coordinate?

If the coordination required to stop AGI is too hard, that does not answer whether we should try.

2

Should we prevent bad actors?

If malicious actors would defeat coordination, that does not answer whether we should oppose them.

3

Should we welcome or oppose the change?

What attitudes we take to AGI’s inevitability also have downstream implications for other actions.

02 — Quick definitions

AGI and superintelligence

AGI ≠ ASI

1

Human-Level AGI

For years this was regarded as an implausible stopping point (my first conversation about AI with Marcus Hutter!). But maybe now seems more realistic?

2

Superhuman AGI

Just AGI+++? Do we have to go full Yudkowsky nanobots etc? Very hard to imagine what this could be. But can at least bucket it by contrast with human-level.

3

Is there a meaningful choice?

When figuring out what future you want to build: it’s possible to say yes to human-level but no to superhuman. It’s also coherent to say no to human-level because it provides no stopping-point before superintelligence.

03 — Freedom & justification

Should individuals’ basic liberal rights constrain state attempts to prevent them building AGI?

AGI and freedom

Libertarian Political liberal Classical liberal Majoritarian

The state may permissibly prevent people from trying to build AGI

  1. Never, no matter the risks.
  2. Only if building AGI incurs existential risk (at what probability?).
  3. Only if building AGI incurs existential or catastrophic risk.
  4. Only if building AGI incurs existential or catastrophic risk, or significant risk to public values.
  5. Only if building AGI incurs existential or catastrophic risk, or significant risk to public values or privately held values.
  6. If the majority (or otherwise appropriately constituted polity) positively choose to prohibit building AGI.

03 — Freedom & justification

A separate decision

AGI—but not superintelligence?

Yes to AGI; no to superintelligence

AGI may be built if specified conditions are met, but superintelligence should not be built. Is this a feasible outcome?

No to both

We cannot stop superintelligence once AGI exists, and we cannot build AGI sufficiently safely.

Yes to both, if safe

Benefits outweigh risks if AGI and superintelligence can be built safely.
