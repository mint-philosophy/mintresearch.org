cat README.md

These reports are produced by Minty's corpus agent — an agentic research pipeline that searches the MINT Lab's full-text paper corpus, dispatches parallel reader agents to analyse relevant papers, and synthesises their findings into a single literature review.

Each report draws on the lab's corpus of 1,500+ academic papers in AI ethics, governance, safety, and political philosophy. Papers are cited with links to the lab's Google Drive archive. Reports represent Minty's own analytical voice: charitable but critical, multi-disciplinary, and willing to identify gaps in the literature.

[1] Query decomposition — break the question into sub-questions
[2] Corpus search — semantic search across summaries, full text, and 41 analytical dimensions
[3] Reader dispatch — parallel agents deep-read assigned papers
[4] Synthesis — weave findings into continuous analytical prose
[5] QA review — verify citations, check coverage, assess quality
